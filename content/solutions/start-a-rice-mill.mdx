## The problem

Farmers often sell paddy cheaply because there is no mill nearby, or because hand-processed rice comes out with stones and broken grains. A small mill close to the farmers lets you offer a milling service, or mill and sell your own clean rice.

## What you need

A combined rice mill is the heart of the business. The SB10 cleans, hulls and whitens paddy in one machine. A destoner removes the stones and sand that customers notice straight away, and a bucket elevator saves lifting grain between machines. You can start with the mill alone and add the others as you grow.

## Power options

- **Mains electricity:** the SB10 uses an 11 kW motor. Check that your supply can carry that load before you choose this route.
- **Diesel:** where the grid is weak or absent, the SB10 can run from a 15 hp diesel engine, or you can choose the SB10D, which comes coupled to one.

## Space and power considerations

The SB10 is about 230 kg and stands about 1.7 m tall, so plan a firm floor and enough height. Leave room to bring paddy in, bag rice out and handle husk and bran. Think about where customers will wait and how trucks or tricycles will reach you.
