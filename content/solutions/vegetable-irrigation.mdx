## The problem

Vegetables need steady water, and carrying it by bucket limits how much land you can farm. A pump lets you keep growing through the dry season, when vegetables are often scarcer.

## What you need

For many vegetable plots near a river, well or dam, a portable 3-inch petrol pump and lay-flat hose is a practical start. The hose rolls up for storage and carries water from the pump to your beds. If you run drip lines or fill an overhead tank from mains power, an electric pressure pump may suit you better.

## Power options

- **Petrol:** portable and simple, so it suits smaller plots and moving between water sources.
- **Diesel:** larger pumps suit bigger fields and long pumping hours.
- **Electric:** pressure pumps suit fixed sites with a mains supply.

## Space and power considerations

Before you choose, measure your land, the distance from your water source to the far end of your field, and the height the water must rise. Suction lift matters too, because a pump can only draw water from so far below it. Send these details to our sales team and they can advise on pump and hose sizes.
