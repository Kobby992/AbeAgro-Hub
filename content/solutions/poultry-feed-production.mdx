## The problem

Feed is one of the biggest costs of keeping birds. Making your own gives you more control over ingredients and supply, and you may be able to sell feed to other farmers nearby.

## What you need

A hammer mill grinds maize, soybean and other ingredients, and is the standard first machine for feed. A flat-die pelletizer then presses mixed meal into firm pellets, which also suit fish, pigs and rabbits. If you keep cattle, sheep or goats as well, a chaff cutter chops forage and crushes grain. Ask an animal nutrition adviser or extension officer about the right recipe for your birds, because it changes as they grow.

## Power options

- **Electric motors:** hammer mills typically use 5.5–22 kW and pelletizers 7.5–22 kW, so check your supply can carry the load.
- **Diesel engine:** ask our sales team about driving your mill from a diesel engine where mains power is weak.

## Space and power considerations

Keep ingredients dry and off the floor, and leave room to mix, cool and bag. A hammer mill makes noise and dust, so choose a well-ventilated spot away from your birds. Check the die size on a pelletizer, because it sets the pellet size.
