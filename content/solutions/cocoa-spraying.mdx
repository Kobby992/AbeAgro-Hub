## The problem

Cocoa trees are tall and their canopies dense, so a hand sprayer struggles to reach the pods where pests and disease do their damage. Spraying large plots by hand is also slow, tiring work.

## What you need

A motorized mist blower uses a fast stream of air to carry fine droplets deep into the canopy. It is carried as a backpack, so one person can move through the farm. A brush cutter keeps the ground between trees clear, which makes the farm easier to walk and spray.

## Power options

- **Petrol:** both machines run on small petrol engines, so they work anywhere on the farm. Follow the manual for fuel and oil mixing on two-stroke engines.

## Space and power considerations

Always wear gloves, a mask, eye protection and long clothes when you spray, and keep other people away. Do not spray in strong wind. For which product to use and how much, follow the product label or ask your extension officer. Rinse the tank and lines after each use, and store fuel and chemicals safely, away from food and children.
