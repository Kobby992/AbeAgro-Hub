## The problem

Many households bring maize, millet and other grains to be ground for a small fee. Where the nearest mill is far away or always busy, a kiosk in a market or busy neighbourhood can meet a steady need.

## What you need

A corn mill handles dry maize, millet, sorghum and rice. If your customers also bring moist foods such as pepper, tomato or beans, look at the wet and dry mini mill, and ask our sales team to confirm its wet capability for what you plan to grind. You will also need a power source, unless the mill comes with one.

## Power options

- **Electric motor:** usually the cheapest to run where the supply is reliable. Check your supply can carry the motor size.
- **Diesel engine:** keeps you grinding through power cuts and away from the grid.
- **Petrol engine:** the mini mill runs from a small petrol engine, which suits a smaller kiosk.

## Space and power considerations

Choose a spot customers can reach easily and where noise and dust will not trouble neighbours. Give the mill a firm base, shade and good airflow. Keep belts guarded, and keep customers clear of the machine while it runs.
